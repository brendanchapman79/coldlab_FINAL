Science applied where it matters most.

Cold Lab is the research and consultancy initiative of Dr Brendan Chapman, forensic scientist and associate professor at Murdoch University's School of Medical, Molecular & Forensic Sciences.

We work with investigators, victim advocates, and research partners to bring rigorous, contemporary forensic science to cases that need it most.
