science applied where it matters most.

[cold_lab] is the research and consultancy initiative of Dr Brendan Chapman, forensic scientist and associate professor at Murdoch University's [School of Medical, Molecular & Forensic Sciences](link:https://www.murdoch.edu.au/schools/medical-molecular-and-forensic-sciences).

we work with investigators, victim advocates, and research partners to bring rigorous, contemporary forensic science to cases that need it most.
