## ccr@murdoch
Dr Brendan Chapman is the director of coldcasereview@murdoch, a think tank of the brightest forensic science students working to assist in the progression of cold cases. having investigated some of the highest profile cases in western australia, Brendan mentors the cold case review team in cutting edge forensic science techniques, case file review and open source intelligence methods.

we work across borders, providing assistance and collaborative networks of expertise in australia, the united states and europe through our inaugural partnership in the international cold case project, run by amber alert EU.

we welcome law enforcement from any jurisdiction to reach out to discuss how our techniques can assist.
